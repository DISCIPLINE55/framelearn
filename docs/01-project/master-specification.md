# FrameLearn — Master Specification

## Project Identification
- **Project Name**: FrameLearn
- **Full Title**: A Web-Based Photography Portfolio, Client Service and Learning Platform for an Independent Photographer
- **Project Type**: Final-Year BSc Information Technology Education Project
- **Project Team**:
  1. Ismail Ibrahim Mensah

---

## 1. Executive Summary
FrameLearn is a specialized web application designed for an independent professional photographer to showcase high-quality work, manage client bookings and private gallery delivery, and offer structured educational resources to aspiring photographers.

---

## 2. Brand Identity & Design Language
FrameLearn operates under a strict, approved visual identity:
- **Primary Navy (`#10212B`)**: Dominant contrast, headers, footers, primary headings, key controls.
- **Sage Green (`#8FA464`)**: Brand accents, secondary actions, selected states, highlights.
- **Light Cream (`#EFFBDD`)**: Main background surfaces, soft section containers, visual breathing room.

Design Principles:
- Photography-first: The UI framing lets photography take center stage.
- Editorial & Spacious: Clean typography and subtle borders over heavy drop shadows.
- Responsive & Accessible: WCAG 2.1 AA compliant contrast and mobile-first responsive layout.

---

## 3. Incremental Development Strategy
Development follows a gated milestone model with strict lecturer reviews between phases:
- **Milestone 001**: Foundation, Architecture & Design System (CURRENT)
- **Milestone 002**: Authentication & User Profiles Foundation
- **Milestone 003**: Photography Portfolio & Services Showcase
- **Milestone 004**: Client Booking System & Scheduling
- **Milestone 005**: Private Client Galleries & Proofing
- **Milestone 006**: Learning Platform & Educational Content
- **Milestone 007**: Admin & Photographer Dashboard
- **Milestone 008**: System Integration, E2E Testing & Final Deployment Review
